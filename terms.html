Domain and hosting

The first year of your .com domain and hosting is included. I register the domain, set up hosting, connect the domain and turn on SSL, then transfer both accounts into your name at handover. After year one, you renew the domain on your own Namecheap account at the then-current .com renewal price, which is $20.18 today including the ICANN fee. Hosting for these sites stays free on the plan I use unless the site outgrows it.

The clock starts once the first payment is paid and your text, logo, and photos are in. Slow feedback moves the date.
