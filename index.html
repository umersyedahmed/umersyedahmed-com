About me

I have 4+ years experience building web applications with Angular and Node.js.

Much of my work is API development. I build REST endpoints, connect outside services such as lab partners and email providers, and keep data in sync between systems. I write code other developers can read and maintain.

I am currently building Qissati, an AI-powered Arabic vocabulary app. It is still in progress.

In progress

Qissati

An Arabic vocabulary app. Qissati writes a short story from the learner’s saved words, then suggests new words after each story. The backend is Node.js and MongoDB. I am also designing the brand and wordmark. It is not finished, and it is not a client project.
