About me

I have 4+ years experience building web applications with Angular and Node.js.

Much of my work is API development. I build REST endpoints, connect outside services such as lab partners and email providers, and keep data in sync between systems. I write code other developers can read and maintain.

I am currently building Qissati, an AI-powered Arabic vocabulary app. Qissati uses AI to generate short stories from a learner’s saved vocabulary, and then suggests new words after every story. The backend is Node.js and MongoDB. I designed the brand and wordmark.

    I develop and maintain a customer-facing health and wellness platform for employer wellness programs. The stack is Angular, NestJS, MongoDB, and an NX monorepo. People register for on-site screening and flu events, view screening results and vaccination records, enroll in incentive programs, and compete in a virtual walking challenge on a U.S. map through a fitness tracker.

    I build responsive UI, including touch-based signature capture, scroll-gated consent flows, and dynamic PDFs from a custom HTML-to-document renderer. Admin tools let non-technical staff manage incentives, mailed reports, and a search-based user lookup that replaced large list loads.     I sync application data to a SQL database for Looker Studio dashboards, and I check that the reported figures match the source data. I fixed NgRx state race conditions, hardened API routes with field whitelists, and resolved timezone and data-export bugs.

How a project would run

Simpler takes about 2 weeks. More advanced takes about 4 weeks. The clock starts once the deposit is paid and your text, logo and photos are in. Slow feedback moves the date. This demo button does not start the clock.

  1. Brief

    You would share what you do, who the site is for, and which pages you have in mind. The clock would not start from this demo button.

  2. Plan

    I write down what the build includes and what it does not, including the page list: up to 5 pages on Simpler, up to 10 on More advanced.

  3. Build

    I build the agreed pages and check them on a phone, tablet and desktop. The homepage design is approved before the build. On the More advanced plan, inner-page designs are approved first too.

  4. Review

    You review the work and send one consolidated list of changes within the agreed scope. Simpler includes 2 rounds. More advanced includes 3. Extra rounds are $100 each.

  5. Launch and handover

    The site goes live, then I hand it over. On final payment, you own the finished site: its code, design and content made for your project, plus your domain and hosting accounts. Support after launch is 30 days on Simpler and 60 days on More advanced.
